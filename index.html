LionHogTech

September 18–20, 2026 · Online

Thank you to everyone who made LionHogTech 2026 a success! High school and undergraduate students from Arkansas and Missouri took a real agricultural dataset from Bayer and turned it into creative, data-driven solutions. Brought to you by the University of Arkansas Biological and Agricultural Engineering department and the MSSU CRIC Club.

About

Main Event

LionHogTech began as a collaboration between the MSSU CRIC Club (Cognitive Robotics and IoT Consortium) at Missouri Southern State University and the Smart Agricultural and Food Engineering Lab at the University of Arkansas. This year it grew into a cross-state event, organized by the University of Arkansas Biological and Agricultural Engineering department and co-organized by MSSU, bringing together high school and undergraduate students from Arkansas and Missouri around AI, agriculture, data science, and innovation.

For 2026, LionHogTech joined forces with the AI in Agriculture Symposium, organized by the Center for Agricultural Data Analytics, Division of Agriculture, University of Arkansas. Teams worked with mentors on a real-world dataset provided by Bayer, and the top three teams went on to present their work at the symposium.

How It Worked

AI in Agriculture Symposium

2nd Annual AI in Agriculture Symposium

September 21, 2026 · Don Tyson Center for Agricultural Sciences, Fayetteville, AR

Our three winning teams took the stage and presented their solutions to researchers, educators, and industry leaders, alongside the top teams from the AI in Ag Hackathon for graduate and senior undergraduate students.

Applied AI in Agriculture

Speakers from Arkansas, Nebraska, Arizona, and Georgia, from academia and industry

Topics included robotics that inspect and sort in real time, computer vision for plant architecture, and satellite remote sensing that helps farmers make decisions.

Organized By

Center for Agricultural Data Analytics, Division of Agriculture, University of Arkansas
